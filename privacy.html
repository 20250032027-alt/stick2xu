Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

In short: we only collect what we need to make your order and hand it to you at school. Only the student sellers see it. We never sell it, and we delete your pictures after your order is done.

Stick2It is a small shop run by students for XU uptown students in Cagayan de Oro. It isn't an official school service. This page explains what we collect when you use stick2it.me, and why.

What we collect

  • Your name, grade, and section, so we can find you and match your order at pickup.
  • Your school Google account email (if you sign in with Google), to confirm you're an XU student. We only get your name and email from Google, nothing else.
  • The pictures and files you upload, so we can print your stickers, pins, keychains, posters, or prints.
  • Your order details: what you ordered, the price, your pickup day, time, and gate, how you paid, and a GCash reference number if you paid by GCash.
  • A few things saved on your own phone or computer (in your browser), like your last order code and your cart, so you don't have to type them again. These stay on your device.

Who sees it

  • Only the student sellers who run Stick2It, to review, print, and hand over your order.
  • Our order details are stored with Supabase, the service that runs our database, and the website is hosted by Vercel.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone else, and we don't use it for ads.
  • We won't post your designs or photos anywhere (like our Facebook or TikTok) unless you clearly say yes first.

How long we keep it

  • Pictures are deleted 30 days after your order is done or cancelled.
  • Documents for prints are deleted 3 days after pickup.
  • Order records (name, section, what you ordered, totals) are kept so we can keep track of sales and pickups. Ask us anytime and we'll delete yours.

Your choices

  • You can cancel an order before it's printed, from "My order" on the website.
  • You can ask us to delete your order and pictures at any time.
  • If you're under 18, it's a good idea to let a parent or guardian know you're ordering.

Contact us

Message us on our Facebook page, or talk to us at pickup.
